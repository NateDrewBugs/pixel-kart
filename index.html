Put it on your Home Screen

iPhone / iPad: open Play Now in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen and works offline.
Android: open Play Now in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
Computer: just press Play Now. In Chrome or Edge you can also install it from the icon at the end of the address bar.
